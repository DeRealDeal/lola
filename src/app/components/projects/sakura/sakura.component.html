SAKURA

A restaurant specially thought for women. We designed a space that combines japanese culture with the female essence, creating a relaxing environment to enjoy sushi or a cocktail with good companionship. This project strived to be unique, from the mobiliary design to the material selection, offering our clients a place they always want to come back.

MATERIALS

The materials that bring this project to life include:

  • Concrete
  • WPC pannels
  • Wallpaper
  • Wood and Melamine

The use of pink tones, flowers, wallpapers, and the warmth from wood evoque an intimate connection with the female essence, offering an experience both visual and emotional
