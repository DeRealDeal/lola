El10TV

The design project for El10Tv's news program reflects the channel's vision and mission of being recognized in the Northwestern region of Argentina, projecting professionalism, and strong connection with its viewers. This is symbolized through a combination of technology and a directional journey in design. Geometric, sharp-angled panels provide directionality while contrasting with the backdrop's organic patterns. This dynamic design combining technology and guiding viewers through an informative journey.

MATERIALS

The materials that bring this project to life include:

  • Melamine, MDF, hardboard.
  • Vinyl wall and floor graphics.
  • LED light strips.

Each of these elements play a crucial role in shaping and enhancing the overall design.
